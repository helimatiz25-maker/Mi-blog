Critical Thinking & Media Literacy

Is a very important skill in dily life and also in the formation of a SENA apprentice.

📅 April 16, 2026

In my opinion, it is a not enouh to accept all the information that we see on internet or in social networks. Although many publications look true, they are not always reliable.

Today there are many pages and people that share false or incomplete information. seeing this situaticn, it is necessary to learn to analyze that we read before believing it or sharing it.

:Having found a news or a publication, we should ask ourselves Who wrote it? Where did it come from? does, it have proof? However, not everything that we find is correct. camparing different sources and checking reliable pages, we can avoid mistakes, can't we?

Although learning this skill can take time, i think it is worth it. Media literacy helps us to recognize fake news and misleading content.

In conclusion, critical thinking is a tool that all of us should develop, because it helps us make better decisions in study, work and daily life.
